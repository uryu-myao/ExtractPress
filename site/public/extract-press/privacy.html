Extract Press

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Your coffee records stay on your phone. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

Extract Press is a coffee logging app for iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you log — cups, ratings, taste notes, beans, gear, cafés and photos — is stored in a database and a folder on your own device. None of it is uploaded. We cannot see it, and we hold no copy of it.

There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

What leaves your device

Two things, and only when you use the features that need them.

Café search. When you search for a café, the text you type is sent to Apple's MapKit service so it can return matching places. If you have allowed location access, your approximate location is sent with it so that nearby results rank first. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy. Nothing from your coffee records is included.

Email you send us. If you tap Send feedback, your mail app opens with a message addressed to us. We receive what you choose to write and the address you send it from. Nothing from your records is attached.

That is the complete list. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to photograph a bag of beans. The photo is processed and stored on your device.
  • Location — optional. Used only to rank café search results by distance. The app works without it; you can type a café name instead.
  • Notifications — optional. Reminders are scheduled by your phone and delivered by your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.

Choosing a photo from your library uses Apple's photo picker, which hands the app only the picture you pick.

Purchases

Extract Press is free. You can leave an optional tip through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. A tip unlocks nothing — the app is the same before and after.

Backups and exports

When you export your records or create a backup, the app writes a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely your choice. We are not involved and receive nothing.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the database and every photo with it. Inside the app you can delete individual cups and bags, and reset your taste profile.

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Extract Press is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
