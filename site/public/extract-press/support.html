Common questions

Where is my data kept?

On your phone, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy for the detail.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Profile and tap Export everything. Save the file somewhere you can reach it. On the new phone, install the app and tap Restore from a backup before you log anything — restoring only works while the app is still empty.

A full device backup through iCloud or a computer also carries the app's data across.

Why does an axis say "Not noted yet"?

Because no cup has recorded it yet. The four taste axes are optional on every cup — note the ones you actually noticed and leave the rest alone. An axis you skip is left out of the calculation rather than counted as middling.

When does my taste profile lock in?

When every axis you have used has ten notes behind it. Before that it stays marked as early signs, because a handful of cups is mostly noise.

Can I change a cup's rating or taste axes afterwards?

No. Each cup stores what it did to your profile at the time it was saved, and editing those values would make that history untrue. The bag, the gear, the date and your own words can all be edited, and a cup can be deleted outright.

Does the app need an internet connection?

Only to search for a café. Everything else works offline, including logging, your taste profile, photos and backups.

I left a tip. What did it unlock?

Nothing, and that is on purpose. The app is free and complete. A tip just pays for a coffee at this end.
